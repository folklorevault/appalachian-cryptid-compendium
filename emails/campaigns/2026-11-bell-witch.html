The Bell Witch
For four years, something lived in a Red River farmhouse. It sang hymns, quoted two preachers' sermons at once, argued scripture with the ministers who came to test it, and slapped children in their beds. When a neighbor asked if it was "Old Kate Batts' witch," it said yes. The name stuck.

A note on the record
The Bell Witch gets called the best-documented haunting in American history. It's really the most thoroughly told. Nearly every version traces back to a single 1894 book, built on a family manuscript nobody else has ever seen. The file walks through what holds up, what doesn't, and why Andrew Jackson probably never stopped by.
Family event real. Death real. Origin of voice undetermined.
